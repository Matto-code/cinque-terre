Getting there

Direct trains, no advance booking needed

Trains run roughly every 15–30 minutes between Rapallo and Monterosso, but services direct to Riomaggiore run less frequently — journey time is around 45 minutes to just over an hour depending on the train. No need to pre-book regional trains — just check the exact time on the Trenitalia app the night before and buy at the station.

Shops and cafes in the villages generally open around 8:30–9am. Starting at Riomaggiore means arriving a little later than you would into Monterosso, so this plan departs Rapallo a touch earlier to compensate.

Buy the Cinque Terre Treno Card at the first station you arrive at — unlimited hop-on trains between all five villages for the day.

Going in reverse — Riomaggiore first — means you hit Riomaggiore and Manarola before the midday cruise-crowd surge, and you finish the day in Monterosso, which has the best connections home (including faster Intercity options). The trade-off: getting there in the first place is more likely to be a slower regional train, since express services skip Riomaggiore entirely.
Corniglia is skipped on this plan — it sits high on a cliff with no direct sea access and requires a long climb up from the station, without much payoff once you're there.

Buying tickets

Rapallo ↔ Riomaggiore/Monterosso: buy via the Trenitalia app or website the night before — no booking fees on regional tickets, and it avoids station queues in the morning. Self-service machines at Rapallo station work too if you're not tight on time.

Cinque Terre Treno Card (unlimited hops between all 5 villages for the day): this is separate from your Rapallo tickets. Buy it online in advance through the Trenitalia app or the official park site (card.parconazionale5terre.it) — station queues for this specific card can be long in peak season. Get it before your first village-to-village hop.

E-tickets bought via app don't need validating. Only paper tickets bought at a counter need validating at the little yellow machines on the platform before boarding.

Riomaggiore

~8:00 – 9:45am
8:00am

Colorful houses & coastal path

Explore the cliffside village while it's still quiet, ahead of the midday crowds. If the Via dell'Amore coastal path to Manarola is open, it's the flattest and most scenic walk in Cinque Terre — worth checking on arrival, since you'll use it (or the train) to move on shortly.

Swimming: a small pebble beach and harbor swim right by the station — good for a quick dip, best for confident swimmers since it's near the ferry stop.

Panini e Affini — outstanding sandwiches, great value, near-perfect reviewsStandout
BRITTA Take Away — smoothies, avocado toast, good for an early biteCheap
Gelateria O.S — homemade, quiet, up a climb but reviewers say worth it (try the stracciatella)Gelato
Bar Zorza — casual bar/bites right by the station, good trofie al pestoBar
BRITTA closes early (around 3–4pm) and isn't open every day — but works well for a morning stop on this order.

Manarola — lunch

~10:00 – 11:45am
10:00am

Lunch by the harbor

Still ahead of the crowd peak here. Casual seafood and focaccia along the water, then photos at the rocks or a short walk up the vineyard path if anyone wants to stretch their legs.

Swimming: the standout spot in all of Cinque Terre — a ladder into deep, clear water right off the harbor rocks, good for jumping/diving. There's also a quieter cove nearby better for snorkeling.

Il Discovolo — vegan/gluten-free friendly, great focaccia, right by a swimming spotCheap
Pizzeria La Cambusa — cheap, fast, good focaccia stracchinoCheap
Gelateria Sorbetteria 5 Terre — widely called the best gelato in Cinque Terre, extensive vegan/GF optionsGelato
Cantina Marinella — cozy wine bar, tasting flights with local snacks, run by the wonderful MariaBar

Vernazza

~12:00 – 2:00pm
12:00pm

Harbor & castle

The most photogenic village — climb up to Doria Castle for the classic postcard view over the harbor. This is where the crowds start to build, so budget a bit of patience for the busiest spots.

Swimming: a small sand beach right in the harbor plus flat rocks for sunbathing — the calmest, most sheltered water on the route.

Il Pirata delle 5 Terre — coffee/pastry stop, try the pistachio croissantStandout
Pippo a Vernazza — pesto pasta & focaccia, takeaway, great valueCheap

Monterosso al Mare

~2:15 – 4:00pm
2:15pm

Beach & wind-down

The only village with a proper sandy beach — a good note to end the day on. Swim, relax, grab a last focaccia before heading back. Monterosso also has the best onward train connections, including faster Intercity services.

Swimming: the easiest, most relaxed beach day of the four — free public sections plus paid loungers if anyone wants sun beds rather than towels on rocks.

Heading back

~4:00 – 5:00pm

Train from Monterosso back to Rapallo — this is the best-connected leg of the day, with more frequent and faster services than the outbound trip. Check the app for the next direct departure. Aim to be back in Rapallo by early evening, in time to freshen up before dinner.
